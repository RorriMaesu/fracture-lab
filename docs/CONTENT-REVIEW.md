# Question bank and academic review

The release contains 336 prompts in 84 concept families: 240 fracture, classification, repair, and cell prompts; 96 supporting Chapter 6 prompts. Each family has recognition, recall or sequence, discrimination, and explanation tasks. A session excludes other tasks from a family already selected. Fresh practice prioritizes unseen prompts, then less-exposed families. Strict unseen practice stops when its eligible pool is exhausted; no finite bank guarantees endless novelty.

The content is authored study material informed by the supplied Chapter 6 and its public OpenStax sections. Sources are mapped per family and preserved in saved attempts. Questions are not copied from textbook review questions. The diagrams are original SVG learning schematics, not diagnostic images or textbook scans.

Keys are checked automatically for structural consistency. Independent anatomy-instructor review is still recommended; it has not occurred. Healing intervals are approximate textbook intervals. Reduction describes the alignment procedure; open/closed fracture describes skin status. Skin status and geometry can coexist. Spelling tolerance must not collapse osteoblast into osteoclast or other valid distinct anatomical terms.

For a content correction, record the question ID, quoted issue, source section, and proposed corrected key/rubric. Update the bank version when grading meaning changes. Historical attempts retain their original question snapshots and bank version.

Question source registry: app/sources.js. Original concepts and criteria: app/concepts.js. Interaction variants and synonyms: app/bank.js.
